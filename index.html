I build production systems where accuracy and accountability decide whether software can be trusted: retrieval-augmented generation, multi-agent AI assistants, and validation logic that checks automated decisions before they affect real data. I care about making these capabilities usable by organizations with small technical teams, such as local governments and community service providers, through auditable design, clear documentation, and shared knowledge.
